# List-building sourcing rubric

Score each lever 1–5 (5 = strong). No industry % benchmarks — judgment against the rubric.

| Lever | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICP fit | Tight firmographic + role filters tied to a real buyer | "Anyone at any company" / role guessed from title text |
| Motion fit | Motion matches the trigger and seed data on hand | Database dump when the whole point was a time-sensitive signal |
| Data freshness | Contacts verified recently; freshness threshold stated | Multi-year-old export, no last-verified date |
| Coverage vs precision | Right trade for the goal; gaps named, not hidden | Chasing 100% coverage with unverified rows, or 5 perfect rows when volume is needed |
| Dedupe / suppression | Deduped against CRM/SEP; unsubscribes and do-not-contact honored | Re-loads people already in sequence or previously opted out |
| Compliance / consent | Sourcing + region + consent regime considered | "Scrape it all" with no regard for terms or privacy law |

**Weakest lever** drives the fix focus. A single ✗ on Compliance or Suppression outweighs high scores elsewhere — never trade those for volume.
