# CTA patterns

## Soft vs hard (defaults)

| Type | Example shape | Relative reply rate (directional) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft | Interest / relevance check | higher |
| Hard | Book a demo / schedule now | lower |

Soft asks tend to outperform hard asks on cold touch 1 (often several-fold). Prefer soft unless the user explicitly requests a later-touch hard ask.

## One CTA only

- Exactly **one** ask per message.
- One CTA tends to outperform stacked asks — competing CTAs dilute response (directional).
- CTA length: **< ~15 words**, on its **own line**.

## Micro-commitment ladder

Sell the **next** commitment, not the product.

| Step | Ask | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Interest | Soft yes/no or relevance check | Touch 1 default |
| 2. Resource | Offer a give (audit, template, teardown) | After interest or when proof is thin |
| 3. 15-min | Short call framed as a **give** | After engagement |
| 4. Breakup | Graceful close; permission to stop | After silence |

Never jump to “book a 30-min demo” on touch 1.

## Soft CTA templates (pick one)

Interest:

- `Worth a look, or should I leave this alone?`
- `Is {{pain}} still on your plate this quarter?`
- `Open to a short teardown of {{thing}}, or not a priority?`

Resource:

- `Want me to send the 5-bullet checklist we used for {{peer type}}?`
- `Happy to share the before/after on {{metric}} — useful?`

15-min (later touch):

- `If useful, I can walk you through the {{give}} in 15 minutes — interested?`

Breakup:

- `If this isn’t relevant, reply “no” and I’ll close the loop.`

## Hard CTAs (avoid on touch 1)

Banned or deferred:

- Book a 30-min demo
- Here’s my calendar
- Reply YES
- Stacked: “reply / book / download / intro me to X”

## Framing calls as a give

Bad: “Can we hop on a call so I can show you the product?”

Good: “If helpful, I can show the 3 gaps we usually find in {{area}} in 15 minutes — no deck.”

## CTA checklist

- [ ] Soft on touch 1
- [ ] Single ask
- [ ] < ~15 words
- [ ] Standalone line
- [ ] Matches ladder step
- [ ] No “Reply YES”
- [ ] No fabricated urgency / fake scarcity
- [ ] Payoff of replying is clear (what they get)
