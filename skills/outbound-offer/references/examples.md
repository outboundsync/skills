# Offer examples (before → after)

Neutral, hypothetical placeholders only. Never reuse the example metrics or
proof as factual claims; replace them with user-provided evidence or clearly
label the claim as unverified.

## 1. Hard demo ask → soft interest

**Before (weak)**

> We help teams accelerate pipeline with our AI-powered platform. Are you free Thursday for a 30-minute demo?

- Dream Outcome: 2 (vague)
- Likelihood: 1 (no proof)
- Speed to Value: 1
- Ease: 1
- Fails: Likelihood + Ease; stacked product pitch + hard CTA

**After (stronger)**

> Offer: Teams like yours cut first-response lag ~35% in two weeks by fixing owner routing before send volume.
> CTA: `Is reply lag still a pain this quarter, or already solved?`
> Ladder: interest

---

## 2. Feature dump → quantified outcome + peer proof

**Before**

> Our tool has sequencing, A/B tests, warmup, and CRM sync. Want to learn more?

**After**

> Offer: One peer team in your segment lifted positive replies ~2× in 21 days by killing tracking on touch 1 and tightening the ask to a soft yes/no.
> CTA: `Want the 5-line checklist we used, or not useful?`
> Ladder: resource

---

## 3. Stacked CTAs → one soft line

**Before**

> Reply YES, book time here, and download our guide!

**After**

> Offer: 10-minute async teardown of your first touch — metric, proof gap, and CTA.
> CTA: `Open to that teardown, or should I leave it?`
> Ladder: interest → resource

---

## 4. Fabricated urgency → genuine timing (or drop)

**Before**

> Only 2 spots left this week — book now!!!

**After**

> Offer: If you’re hiring SDRs this month, the costly mistake is scaling volume before the offer/CTA is tight.
> CTA: `Still hiring into outbound this month?`
> Ladder: interest  
> Note: only keep scarcity if real and verifiable; otherwise omit.

---

## 5. Call framed as take → call framed as give

**Before**

> Can we schedule a quick call so I can pitch our solution?

**After**

> Offer: I’ll show the 3 offer gaps that usually suppress replies — you keep the notes either way.
> CTA: `If useful later, open to 15 minutes for that walkthrough?`
> Ladder: 15-min (not touch 1 unless prior engagement)

---

## Why-you / why-now / what-you-get (good)

- Why you: You own outbound reply rate for mid-market SDR teams.
- Why now: You’re ramping send volume this quarter; weak offers compound waste.
- What you get by replying: A one-page teardown of outcome, proof, and CTA — no deck.
