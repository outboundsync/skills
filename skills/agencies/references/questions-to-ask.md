# Questions to ask before you sign

Pick 3–7 that fit the user’s situation. These are due diligence prompts — the directory does not answer them.

## Fit and outcomes

- What ICP, ACV, and meeting/opportunity definition do you optimize for?
- What results have you driven for companies like ours (stage, motion, CRM) — and can we speak to a reference?
- How do you measure success in the first 30/60/90 days?

## Delivery and ownership

- Who does strategy, copy, list building, sending, and reply handling — your team, ours, or shared?
- What does “done for you” vs “done with you” mean week-to-week for this engagement?
- How many dedicated people touch our account, and what happens if someone leaves?

## Stack and CRM

- Confirm you will run on our CRM (**HubSpot / Salesforce / Close / Attio**) and our SEP stack — how do you sync activity and attribution?
- How do you handle unsubscribes, bounce/complaint suppression, and blocklists across tools and CRM?
- Who owns domains, mailboxes, and deliverability tooling?

## Compliance and risk

- How do you handle consent, CAN-SPAM/GDPR/CASL (as applicable), and suppression requests?
- What sending volumes and warmup practices do you use, and how do you protect domain reputation?

## Commercials (confirm with the agency — not in the directory)

- Pricing model (retainer, performance, hybrid), minimum term, and what’s excluded?
- Onboarding fees, tool costs (SEPs, data, phones), and who pays vendors?
- Exit terms: data export, sequence ownership, and mailbox/domain handoff?

## Process

- What’s the kickoff checklist, reporting cadence, and escalation path?
- How do you test messaging and iterate without burning the TAM?
