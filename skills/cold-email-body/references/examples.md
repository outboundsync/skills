## 1. Generic → signal-led (removal test)

**Before (~2% reply class)**

> Hi {{first_name}}, I hope this finds you well. We are a best-in-class platform that helps companies like yours leverage outbound. Can we hop on a quick call? Here’s my calendar: {{link}}

Issues: banned opener, sludge, hard CTA, link on touch 1, decorative personalization.

**After (~signal-led class)**

```text
Saw you’re hiring 3 SDRs while send volume is already up on your careers page.

That pattern usually spikes unsubs before reply systems tighten — the fix is usually offer + CTA, not more tools.

Peer teams in your band lifted positive replies ~2× in 21 days by killing touch-1 tracking and using one soft ask.

Is reply quality still the constraint, or is volume the only goal right now?
```

Removal test: delete sentence 1 → email loses why-them → personalization is real.

## 5. Hygiene checklist (touch 1)

- Plain text
- No links, images, attachments
- Open pixel OFF
- Link tracking OFF
- Minimal signature (name + company text only)
- Spintax only for variation insurance, not meaning changes
