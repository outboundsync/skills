# Connection request examples

Neutral placeholders. Never name a specific professional network.

## Generic note (worst) → blank or specific

**Before (generic — kill)**

> Hi {{first_name}}, I’d like to add you to my professional network. Open to connecting?

**Better A — blank**

```text
(blank — no note)
```

**Better B — specific (148 chars)**

```text
Liked your note on fixing owner routing before scaling sends — dealing with the same reply-lag issue with SDR teams. Happy to connect.
```

---

## Pitchy note → clean

**Before**

> We help companies 10x pipeline. Here’s my calendar: {{link}} — let’s chat!

**After**

```text
Saw your comment in the {{group}} thread on touch-1 tracking — agreed on placement risk. Would be good to connect.
```

## Too long (near 300) → compress

**Before (bloated)**

> I’ve been following your content for a while and really appreciate your insights on go-to-market, outbound, tooling, team structure, and hiring. I work in a similar space and would love to share ideas, swap notes, and explore synergies when you have time.

**After**

```text
Your post on killing open pixels for cold sends tracked with what we’re seeing on placement. Mind connecting?
```

When outbound replies stall, do you usually touch copy first or the offer/CTA?

Thanks for connecting! Got 15 minutes this week for a quick call?
